#### Maximilian Schwabe

_PhD Student_

I am a PhD student in physics at RWTH Aachen. In my master studies, I specialized in theoretical biophysics. Dealing with problems from applied machine learning and computational neuroscience during my Bachelor's and Master's thesis got me interested in how to ground a theory of structure formation in neural networks in concepts from statistical physics and dynamical systems theory. Right now, my focus lies on the statistical mechanics of RNNs in the feature-learning regime, as well as in building bridges between neural network theory and neurobiology.
