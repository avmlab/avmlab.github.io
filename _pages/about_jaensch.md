#### David Jänsch

_MSc Student_

I am a Master's student at RWTH Aachen, studying for a double degree in Physics (Quantum Technology) and Data Science (Mathematics and Machine Learning). For my Bachelor's thesis, I employed normalising flows to correct simulated photon data at the CMS experiment. Alongside my studies, I work as a student researcher at Fraunhofer ILT, where I use deep learning for OCT-guided laser applications. Having applied machine learning for years, I am now interested in understanding why it works, which has led me to the statistical physics of neural networks. In my thesis I study teacher–student setups, focusing on what networks in the feature-learning regime can learn from limited data.
